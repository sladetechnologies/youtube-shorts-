Imagine a village where people could SELL TOMORROW!

Every day, people sold their future for money. Next month's salary, next year's harvest... even ten years of their lives!

And the mayor? He LOVED it!

The village became rich. Shops expanded. Parties never stopped.

But there was ONE problem...

Nobody could buy their future back.

Soon, farmers sold their harvests. Workers sold their salaries. And the mayor sold TEN YEARS of future taxes!

Then one morning...

Nobody had anything left to sell.

The market was empty. The farms were abandoned.

But inside the town hall, an old contract revealed the truth.

Every future payment belonged to the buyers.

Even the mayor's salary!

The village hadn't become rich.

It had simply spent its future... pretending it was rich.

And when tomorrow arrived...

Nobody owned it.
